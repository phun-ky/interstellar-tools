# 2.0 release notes <Badge type="tip" text="November 2026" />

![Interstellar Tools 2.0: galaxies at their real distances](/images/interstellar-tools-v2@2x.png)

`types` and `constants` go to **2.0.0**. The other packages, `temporal`,
`equations`, `maps`, `tracking` and `mcp`, get a patch release that moves their
range to the new `types`, and nothing else.

These notes cover what changed between 1.x and 2.0. For the code changes, step
by step, see the
[migration guide from 1.x to 2.0](../upgrading.md#migrating-from-1-x-to-2-0).
Questions? See [getting help](#getting-help).

[[toc]]

## Overview

2.0 is a correction. The galaxy dataset had values that were wrong by orders of
magnitude, and one that described something that doesn't exist: a supermassive
black hole in Triangulum, which observations rule out. Fixing that needed a type
change, `blackHole` optional, and that's a breaking change, so it waited for a
major. The major also removes `MeasureInterface`, which was deprecated before
1.0 and announced for removal in 2.0.

It matters to you if you use `GALAXIES`, or `GalaxyInterface` and
`BlackHoleInterface` in your own data, or still import `MeasureInterface`.
Nothing else changes.

## What's new

### Galaxies at their real distances

Andromeda was 2.5 trillion light-years away in 1.x, a million times too far, and
Triangulum 3 trillion. Both are now at their distances from Cepheid variables,
measured with the Hubble Space Telescope:

| Galaxy     | 1.x                  | 2.0                    | Source              |
| ---------- | -------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------- |
| Andromeda  | 2 537 002 537 000 ly | 2 482 050 ly (761 kpc) | Li et al. 2021      |
| Triangulum | 3 000 003 000 000 ly | 2 739 714 ly (840 kpc) | Breuval et al. 2023 |

Triangulum's distance is still debated: other methods give up to about 880 kpc.

### A black hole only where one is detected

`GalaxyInterface`'s `blackHole` is now optional. Triangulum has no supermassive
black hole: the stars at its centre limit one to about 1500 solar masses
(Gebhardt et al. 2001). In 1.x it had one of 1.5 million.

The black holes that are there have their measured masses, and their radius is
now their Schwarzschild radius, $2GM/c^2$, computed from the mass:

| Black hole      | Mass                      | Radius   | Source                     |
| --------------- | ------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------- |
| Sagittarius A\* | 4.2996 × 10⁶ M☉           | 0.085 au | GRAVITY Collaboration 2024 |
| M31\*           | 1.4 × 10⁸ M☉ (1.1 to 2.3) | 2.76 au  | Bender et al. 2005         |

Each black hole sits at its galaxy's centre, with `x` and `y` 0, as
`BlackHoleInterface` describes. In 1.x, the black hole's `y` held its galaxy's
distance in au. Real 3D positions for the galaxies are planned
([#373](https://github.com/phun-ky/interstellar-tools/issues/373)).

### Diameters of one kind

A galaxy has no sharp edge, so a diameter depends on where you draw it. The
diameters are now all the **D25** diameter, where the disk fades to 25
B-magnitudes per square arcsecond: from the RC3 catalogue for Andromeda (137 570
ly) and Triangulum (56 420 ly), and an estimate from a model of its disk for the
Milky Way (87 410 ly). The stars reach further out than that. The types are now
the de Vaucouleurs types: `SBbc` for the Milky Way, `SA(s)b` for Andromeda and
`SA(s)cd` for Triangulum.

Every value has its source in the dataset's documentation and in
[`NOTICE.md`](https://github.com/phun-ky/interstellar-tools/blob/main/NOTICE.md#data),
and the tests check each one against it.

## What's not changing

- **Everything outside the galaxies.** The planets, moons, stars, asteroids,
  comets, belts and systems keep their shape and values.
- **The other packages' APIs.** `temporal`, `equations`, `maps`, `tracking` and
  `mcp` only move their range to `types` 2.0.
- **Galaxy ids**, `milky-way`, `andromeda` and `triangulum`, and the fields
  other than `blackHole`.

## Deprecated

Nothing new.

## Removed

| API                | Use instead         | Deprecated in |
| ------------------ | ------------------- | ------------- |
| `MeasureInterface` | `DistanceInterface` | 0.63          |

`MeasureInterface` was identical to `DistanceInterface`, so the change is a
rename.

## FAQ

### Do I need to upgrade right away?

No. types and constants 1.x get fixes for high and critical vulnerabilities for
6 months after 2.0, as the
[security policy](https://github.com/phun-ky/interstellar-tools/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
describes. But only 2.0 has the corrected galaxy data.

### How big is the upgrade?

Small. If you don't use `GALAXIES` or `MeasureInterface`, upgrading the packages
is all there is. If you do, check for `blackHole` before reading it, and don't
place galaxies by their black hole's position. The
[migration guide](../upgrading.md#migrating-from-1-x-to-2-0) has a checklist.

Upgrade all the packages together, so they share one copy of `types`.

### Does this change how the MCP server is set up?

No. Its galaxies resource serves the corrected data, so an assistant sees
Triangulum without a black hole.

## Getting help

- **Something doesn't work after upgrading?** Check the
  [migration guide](../upgrading.md#migrating-from-1-x-to-2-0), then
  [open an issue](https://github.com/phun-ky/interstellar-tools/issues) with the
  package versions and a short example.
- **A question?** Open an issue too; questions are welcome.
- **A security problem?** Don't open an issue. Report it privately, as the
  [security policy](https://github.com/phun-ky/interstellar-tools/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
  describes.
- **Every change in detail:** each package's `CHANGELOG.md`.
